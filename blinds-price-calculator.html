    Blinds cost questions

    How much do blinds cost in Vadodara?

    Blinds are priced by area in square feet. Starting prices: roller ₹80, zebra ₹150, wooden ₹250, honeycomb ₹375, Venetian ₹150, Roman ₹360, Shangri-La (triple shade) ₹250 and Vertiduo ₹500 per sq ft. For example, a 5 ft × 6 ft window (60 × 72 inches, 30 sq ft) in zebra blinds is about ₹4,750 with fitting. GST is extra as applicable.

    How is the blind size worked out?

    Width × height in inches ÷ 144 gives square feet. It is rounded up to the next 0.25 sq ft, with a minimum of 12 sq ft per blind.

    What is a pelmet and what does it cost?

    A pelmet is a cover at the top of a roller blind that hides the roller. It adds ₹40 per sq ft to roller and printed roller blinds.

    What do motorized blinds cost?

    One motor per window. Roller, zebra and triple shade motors start from ₹6,500, wooden and honeycomb from ₹12,000 and Vertiduo from ₹14,000. Fitting for a motorized blind is ₹800. The final motor price depends on the model you choose.

    Do you make printed roller blinds?

    Yes. Customized printed roller blinds, often used in temples and to highlight windows, start from ₹170 per sq ft. Pelmet and fitting are extra.
