Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Cube Furnishing handles personal information when you use cubefurnishing.com, use the Cube Furnishing Android app, or contact our showroom in Vadodara, Gujarat.

Information we receive

You can browse this website without giving us your name or contact details. We receive personal information when you choose to share it with us, for example by phone, email, WhatsApp or at our showroom. This can include:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • your address, room or window measurements, and photos of your space
  • the products you are interested in, your orders, payments and messages to us

The enquiry form and enquiry list

The form on this website prepares a message on your device. It does not send your details to us automatically. We receive them only if you send the message by email or another method you choose.

Your enquiry list is saved in your own browser so that it is still there when you return. You can remove items from the list or clear your browser's site data at any time.

How we use your information

  • to answer enquiries and prepare quotes
  • to arrange consultations, measurements, delivery and installation
  • to process orders and payments, and provide after-sales support
  • to request feedback, including a link to leave a Google review after your work is complete
  • to keep records required by law

When information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to serve you or meet a legal duty, such as with our installers, delivery partners, suppliers, payment or email providers, and government authorities.

Services this website uses

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, when pages load. Our email runs on Google Workspace.

We have not added advertising or analytics tracking to this website. Links to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Google Maps and Google reviews take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply there.

The Cube Furnishing app

Our Android app "Cube Furnishing" (package in.cubefurnishing.quote) is a work tool for our team to prepare quotations, order and stitching sheets, receipts, leads, job cards and installation work.

  • Everything entered in the app (quotes, client names, phone numbers, addresses, measurements, photos, payments, staff logins) is stored only on the phone it is used on. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics and does not use the advertising ID.
  • Information leaves the phone only when the user chooses to share a PDF (for example on WhatsApp), call a number, or save a backup or export file.
  • Permissions: Internet (to load fonts), Phone calls (one-tap calling), Notifications (task and work reminders), and running after restart (to keep reminders). Photos and backup files are chosen only through Android's own picker.
  • Staff PINs are stored in scrambled (hashed) form. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything it stored.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, including after-sales support and legal or accounting records. We then delete it or stop using it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct or erase it, withdraw consent you have given, or address a complaint. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging furnishing purchases. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us so we can review it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal duties change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
