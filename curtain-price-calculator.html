    Arabian, 3 Pleat, Box Pleat and Roman Blinds

    Curtains hang from a rod or track and open to the sides. Roman blinds are flat fabric panels that fold up as you raise them.

    Which lining (astar)

    Satin lining softens light and protects the fabric. Blackout lining blocks light, ideal for bedrooms. No lining keeps sheer fabrics light and airy.

    Curtain cost questions

    How much does a curtain cost in Vadodara?

    It depends on the window size, the fabric you choose and the lining (astar). For example, a 5 ft × 7 ft window (60 × 84 inches) with ₹300 per metre fabric and no lining comes to about ₹4,000 for an Arabian / 3 pleat curtain, including stitching, channel and fitting. Enter your own sizes above to see your price.

    How much fabric do I need for curtains?

    You do not need to work it out. The curtain fabric calculator above finds the metres needed from your window width and height, so you only enter the fabric price per metre.

    Are curtain stitching charges included?

    Yes. The price includes fabric, lining (astar), stitching and channel, and fitting. GST is extra as applicable.

    What is the Roman blind price?

    Choose Roman blind in the calculator and enter your window size and fabric price. Roman blinds are priced by the size of the blind, so you see the full cost straight away.
