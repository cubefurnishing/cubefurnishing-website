    Curtain or Roman blind

    Curtains hang from a rod or track and open to the sides. Roman blinds are flat fabric panels that fold up as you raise them.

    Which lining

    Satin lining softens light and protects the fabric. Blackout lining blocks light, ideal for bedrooms. No lining keeps sheer fabrics light and airy.
